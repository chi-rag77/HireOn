[ A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS ]

Before we
get started.

Something else on your mind?

Is HireOn available yet?

HireOn is preparing for launch. Request early access to talk with the team about your workflow and learn about availability.

Who is HireOn being built for?

Both in-house HR teams and recruitment agencies. The workflow brings role creation, candidate screening, structured interviews, and evidence into one place.

Can our team define its own hiring process?

HireOn's workflow is designed around your role criteria, scorecards, and interview rounds. The interactive example above shows how AI can draft a starting point for your team to refine.

Does AI make the final hiring decision?

Your team makes the final decision. AI helps organize information and surface evidence; recruiters and hiring managers review it in context.

How does HireOn contact candidates?

HireOn is designed to support personalized email and WhatsApp outreach, scheduling, and structured AI phone interviews. Candidate consent, opt-out controls, and your team's oversight remain part of the workflow.

What about LinkedIn, Naukri, and our existing data?

The workflow illustrates bringing candidates together from recruiting sources, CSV files, and existing talent pools. Tell us which tools you use so we can confirm the supported import and integration options for your early-access setup.

Are these real candidates and hiring results?

No. The people, scores, counts, and interview events shown here are sample data used to illustrate the product. They are not customer results or testimonials.
