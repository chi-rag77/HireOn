01THE BRIEF

Start with who
you need.

Tell AI what you’re hiring for. Refine the job description, required skills, and criteria that define a strong fit. Those criteria guide the résumé analysis and the interview evaluation.

Define what good looks like.
02THE PROCESS

Set the rounds.
Set the deadline.

Configure the rounds and evaluation criteria before you invite anyone. For example: an AI interview, a skills assessment, and an assignment, all to be completed within three days.

Your process, ready before applications arrive.
03THE FIRST SELECTION

Hundreds of résumés.
Find the right fit.

Bring applications from Naukri, LinkedIn, CSV files, and your talent pool. HireOn analyzes every résumé against your criteria and explains the matches. Your team chooses which candidates to invite.

Review the fit. Select who enters the process.
04THE INVITATION

One invitation.
The whole process.

Send each selected candidate access to all their configured rounds, with a clear completion deadline. HireOn follows up over email and WhatsApp. The interview links expire when the window closes.

Three rounds within three days — in this example.
05CANDIDATES TAKE IT FROM HERE

They complete.
You don’t coordinate.

Candidates work through the AI interviews, assessments, and assignments on their own time, before the deadline. They don’t wait for your team to schedule or approve each round. Once they submit all rounds, HireOn calculates their hiring score automatically.

One submission flow. No hand-off between rounds.
06THE RANKED SHORTLIST

Your top three.
And why they stand out.

HireOn combines the completed rounds into hiring scores and ranks the candidates. Review the strongest three with clear reasons, supporting answers, assessment results, and assignment evidence. Know what makes each person a fit, and what to explore further.

A recommendation you can explain.
07THE FINAL CONVERSATION

Less screening.
Closer to your hire.

Share the shortlist internally, discuss the evidence with your team, and meet the finalists one-on-one. Focus your time on the strongest candidates and closing the hire, instead of working through hundreds of applications.

Your team makes the final hiring decision.

The work flows. The decision stays human.

[ A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS ]

Before we
get started.

Something else on your mind?

Is HireOn available yet?

HireOn is preparing for launch. Request early access to talk with the team about your workflow and learn about availability.

Who is HireOn being built for?

Both in-house HR teams and recruitment agencies. The workflow brings role creation, candidate screening, structured interviews, and evidence into one place.

Can our team define its own hiring process?

Yes. Set the rounds, evaluation criteria, and completion window for each role. In the example above, selected candidates complete an AI interview, a skills assessment, and an assignment within three days, without waiting for approval between rounds. Your team can hold final one-on-one conversations after reviewing the ranked shortlist.

What happens when the interview links expire?

The links stop accepting round submissions after the configured deadline. The three-day window shown above is an example; your team sets the completion window for the role.

When are hiring scores and rankings calculated?

Once a candidate submits all configured rounds, HireOn calculates their hiring score and ranks the completed candidates. The shortlist includes reasons and supporting evidence for the recommendations, so your team can discuss the results and hold final conversations.

Does AI make the final hiring decision?

Your team makes the final decision. AI helps organize information and surface evidence; recruiters and hiring managers review it in context.

How does HireOn contact candidates?

Selected candidates receive access to their configured rounds with a completion deadline. HireOn follows up over email and WhatsApp so candidates can finish independently before their links expire.

What about LinkedIn, Naukri, and our existing data?

The workflow illustrates bringing candidates together from recruiting sources, CSV files, and existing talent pools. Tell us which tools you use so we can confirm the supported import and integration options for your early-access setup.

Are these real candidates and hiring results?

No. The people, scores, counts, and interview events shown here are sample data used to illustrate the product. They are not customer results or testimonials.
